Repaints the main fire truck (the CCF) in **QLD RFS golden yellow** with the Rural Fire Service and Queensland Fire Department logos and **red and white rear chevrons**. It also removes the French lettering as far as the 3D model allows, and the number plates. It swaps the siren and horn for Australian **Hazard 1000** tones, and (new in v1.2) turns all the emergency lights **red and much brighter** with the [Red Beacons](/mods/red-beacons/) mod.

## What it changes

| | |
|---|---|
| Files | `z_QLD_Truck_P.*` (truck paint, chunk 1002) + `z_Hazard_1000_Sounds_P.*` (siren + horn, chunk 1001) + `pakchunk1004-Red_Beacons-Windows_P.*` (red beacons, chunk 1004) |
| Replaces | `T_CCF_Precise_D/ERM/N`, `T_CCF_VariousTiling_D`, `T_CCF_StripedMarking_D`, `T_CCF_TrimSheet_D`, the siren and horn sounds, and the four beacon meshes |

## Known limitations

- The truck's paint uses **shared colour blocks**, so some small parts turn yellow along with the body.
- The old side lettering is **part of the 3D model**. It's painted and finished to match the body, but can still be faintly visible in some lighting. New lettering can't be painted there ([why](/reference/ccf-truck/#painting-tips)).

[How to install](/mods/installing/). Want the truck paint without the tones or the red lights? Delete the three `z_Hazard_1000_Sounds_P` or `pakchunk1004-Red_Beacons-Windows_P` files after installing.
