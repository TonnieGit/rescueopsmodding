Repaints the main fire truck (the CCF) in **QLD RFS golden yellow** with the Rural Fire Service and Queensland Fire Department logos and **red and white rear chevrons**. It also removes the French lettering as far as the 3D model allows, and the number plates.

**New in v1.5:** the truck comes fitted out with [ELS](/mods/els/) and the [ELS Light Bar](/mods/els-light-bar/):

- A full-width **red and blue LED light bar** in place of the two front roof beacons, with a **rear traffic advisor**, flash patterns, scene and takedown lights. Every module throws **real light**.
- Recorded **Hazard 1000** sirens (Wail, Yelp, Dual) plus Hi-Lo and Phaser, an air horn, a manual wail and a siren menu. ELS replaces the old Hazard 1000 tones, so the truck no longer includes them.
- The [Red + Blue Beacons](/mods/red-blue-beacons/) stay on the grille and rear: much brighter, alternating red and blue, and throwing **real light** onto the ground, walls, trees and smoke.

## How to use

While driving:

| Key | What it does |
|---|---|
| Lights key | Light bar and beacons on and off (the game's own key) |
| **K** | Siren on and off (the game's own key) |
| **H** | Siren on: air horn while held. Siren off: manual wail |
| **H H** | Double-tap: next siren tone |
| **U** | Lights menu: patterns, traffic advisor, scene, stages, takedown, park mode, lens tint |
| **I** | Sirens menu: Wail, Yelp, Dual, Hi-Lo, Phaser, Auto Cycle, Rumbler, Low Volume |

In a menu, point with the mouse or scroll, then click or release to pick. Right-click goes back. Full details: [ELS](/mods/els/) and [ELS Light Bar](/mods/els-light-bar/).

## Known limitations

- The truck's paint uses **shared colour blocks**, so some small parts turn yellow along with the body.
- The old side lettering is **part of the 3D model**. It's painted and finished to match the body, but can still be faintly visible in some lighting. New lettering can't be painted there ([why](/reference/ccf-truck/#painting-tips)).
- The lights and sirens are on your own screen only. In multiplayer, other players see and hear their own game's truck.

[How to install](/mods/installing/). Prefer [Brighter](/mods/brighter-beacons/) or [Red Beacons](/mods/red-beacons/) on the grille and rear? Install that colour afterwards; the Mod Launcher swaps it in.

:::caution[Updating from v1.4 or earlier]
The Mod Launcher and `Install.bat` remove the old Hazard 1000 files and old beacon files for you. Installing by hand? First delete `z_Hazard_1000_Sounds_P.*`, `pakchunk1004-Red_Blue_Beacons-Windows_P.*` and `pakchunk1004-Red_Beacons-Windows_P.*` from `Wildfire\Content\Paks`.
:::
