Repaints the main fire truck (the CCF) in **QLD RFS golden yellow** with the Rural Fire Service and Queensland Fire Department logos and **red and white rear chevrons**. It also removes the French lettering as far as the 3D model allows, and the number plates. It swaps the siren and horn for Australian **Hazard 1000** tones, and gives it **red and blue** emergency lights, much brighter, with the [Red + Blue Beacons](/mods/red-blue-beacons/) mod: red roof beacons on the left and blue on the right, and alternating red and blue lightheads. **New in v1.4:** every beacon throws **real light** onto the ground, walls, trees and smoke.

## What it changes

| | |
|---|---|
| Files | `z_QLD_Truck_P.*` (truck paint, chunk 1002) + `z_Hazard_1000_Sounds_P.*` (siren + horn, chunk 1001) + `pakchunk1007-Red_Blue_Beacons-Windows_P.*` (red + blue colour) + [Beacon Base and Tonnie Core](/mods/tonnie-core/) (chunks 1004, 1005) |
| Replaces | `T_CCF_Precise_D/ERM/N`, `T_CCF_VariousTiling_D`, `T_CCF_StripedMarking_D`, `T_CCF_TrimSheet_D`, the siren and horn sounds, and the four beacon meshes |

## Known limitations

- The truck's paint uses **shared colour blocks**, so some small parts turn yellow along with the body.
- The old side lettering is **part of the 3D model**. It's painted and finished to match the body, but can still be faintly visible in some lighting. New lettering can't be painted there ([why](/reference/ccf-truck/#painting-tips)).

[How to install](/mods/installing/). Want the truck paint without the tones? Delete the three `z_Hazard_1000_Sounds_P` files after installing. Prefer [Brighter](/mods/brighter-beacons/) or [Red Beacons](/mods/red-beacons/)? Install that colour afterwards; the Mod Manager swaps it in.

:::caution[Updating from v1.3 or earlier]
The Mod Manager and `Install.bat` remove the old beacon files for you. Installing by hand? First delete `pakchunk1004-Red_Blue_Beacons-Windows_P.*` and `pakchunk1004-Red_Beacons-Windows_P.*` from `Wildfire\Content\Paks`, because the new Beacon Base uses the same lights.
:::
