A **first-person view**. See the fire from your firefighter's eyes, and drive the truck from the driver's seat. Press **B** to switch between the game's normal camera and first person; the mod remembers your choice for next time.

## Keys

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **B** | First person on/off |
| **Mouse** | On foot: look and turn, as usual. In a truck: look around the cab |
| Hold **Alt** | Free look on foot: turn your head without turning your body. Let go and the view swings back |
| Double-tap **Alt** | Lock free look on. Tap **Alt** again to unlock |
| Hold **right mouse** | Zoom |

## On foot and in the truck

- **On foot** the camera rides your firefighter's head, so it moves as you walk. Your own body is hidden from you, but its shadow stays.
- **In a fire truck** the camera is fixed in your seat and moves with the truck. It works in every seat: the driver's seat, the front passenger seat and both rear seats.

![First person in the driver's seat of the fire truck, looking through the windscreen](/media/first-person/cab.jpg)

## Settings

In the pause menu, **Settings › General** gets two new rows:

- **First Person FOV**: 70 to 120 (default 90).
- **First Person Head Bob**: 0 to 100 % (default 100 %). Turn it down if walking in first person makes you feel sick.

Your settings are saved and come back next time.

It only changes your own screen. In multiplayer, other players see you as normal.

:::note[Needs ROMF]
First person starts through [ROMF](/mods/tonnie-core/), a small shared file. The [Mod Launcher](/mods/installing/) installs it for you, and the zip and its `Install.bat` include it too.
:::

## Known limitations

- First person appears once a **fire truck** has spawned, which is the case in the lobby and on every mission. The two settings rows only show in the pause menu during a game, not in the main menu.
- Some of the game's own close-up views (for example opening a storage) may show in first person instead of their usual angle. Press **B** for the normal camera if one looks odd.
- A game update can break [ROMF](/mods/tonnie-core/#after-a-game-update). If the game crashes while loading after an update, remove its three files until a new version is out.
