**ELS** gives the main fire truck (the CCF) a proper siren system. The game's siren is replaced by recorded **Australian Hazard 1000** tones, picked from a siren menu while you drive.

- **Tones:** Wail, Yelp and Dual (recorded), plus Hi-Lo and Phaser.
- **Air horn** while the siren is on, and a **manual wail** that winds up and down while the siren is off.
- **Auto Cycle** steps through the tones, the **Rumbler** adds a low rumble, and **Low Volume** turns the sirens down for you only.

ELS doesn't change the lights. Keep your beacons, or add the [ELS Light Bar](/mods/els-light-bar/) for a full LED light bar.

## Controls

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **K** | Siren on/off (the game's own key; ELS plays its own siren) |
| Hold **H** | Siren on: air horn. Siren off: a manual wail that winds up while held |
| **H H** (double-tap) | Next siren tone |
| **I** | Sirens menu |
| **U** | Lights menu (with the [ELS Light Bar](/mods/els-light-bar/)) |

In a menu, point with the mouse or scroll, then click or let go of the key to pick. Right-click goes back. The menus are in French when the game is.

:::note[Replaces Hazard 1000 Tones]
ELS has the Hazard 1000 tones and more, so you don't need [Hazard 1000 Tones](/mods/hazard-1000-tones/) with it. The [Mod Launcher](/mods/installing/) offers to switch you over.
:::

:::note[Needs Tonnie Core v2]
ELS starts through [Tonnie Core](/mods/tonnie-core/) **v2**. The [Mod Launcher](/mods/installing/) installs it for you, and the zip and its `Install.bat` include it too.
:::

## Known limitations

- Siren choices are on your own screen only. In multiplayer, other players hear their own game's siren.
- A game update can break [Tonnie Core](/mods/tonnie-core/#after-a-game-update). If the game crashes while loading after an update, remove its three files until a new version is out.
