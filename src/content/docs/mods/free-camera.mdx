A **free camera** for screenshots and video. Fly anywhere, hide the HUD and change the time of day, all from the keyboard. The camera glides rather than jerks, so slow passes look like a real camera move.

## Keys

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **F8** | Free camera on/off. Your character, or the truck you're driving, stays put while it's on |
| **W A S D** | Move |
| **Q / E** | Down / up |
| **Mouse** | Look |
| **Scroll wheel** | Slower / faster |
| Hold **Shift** | 3× speed |
| Hold **Ctrl** | ¼ speed, for slow, clean passes |
| **F9** | Hide / show the HUD |
| Hold **]** / **[** | Time of day forward / back, about 1 game-hour a second. With **Ctrl**: slowly, for a smooth sunset. With **Shift**: fast |

It only works on your own screen. In multiplayer, other players don't see it.

:::note[Needs Tonnie Core]
The camera starts through [Tonnie Core](/mods/tonnie-core/), a small shared file. The [Mod Manager](/mods/installing/#option-1-mod-manager-easiest) installs it for you, and the zip and its `Install.bat` include it too.
:::

## Known limitations

- The camera appears once a **fire truck** has spawned, which is the case in the lobby and on every mission.
- The time-of-day keys work best in single player or as the host. In someone else's game the host's time can win.
- A game update can break [Tonnie Core](/mods/tonnie-core/#after-a-game-update). If the game crashes while loading after an update, remove its three files until a new version is out.
