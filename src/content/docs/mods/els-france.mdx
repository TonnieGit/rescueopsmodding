French fire-service lights and sirens in one install for the main fire truck (the CCF):

- An **all-blue LED light bar** in place of the two front roof beacons, with a **rear traffic advisor** stick. The warning modules, the rear lights and the **real light** they throw are all blue. The traffic advisor stays amber, and the scene and takedown lights white.
- **Blue grille and rear beacons** ([Beacons: Blue](/mods/brighter-beacons/)), throwing real blue light.
- Five French **deux-tons pompiers** sirens on the sirens menu (**I**), on La 435 Hz / Si 488 Hz.

| Siren | Sound |
|---|---|
| **2 tons** | Air horn, 27 cycles a minute |
| **2 tons électronique** | Electronic, 30 cycles a minute |
| **2 tons ancien** | Older air horn, 25 cycles a minute |
| **2 tons rapide** | Fast, 50 cycles a minute |
| **électronique rapide** | Fast electronic, 55 cycles a minute |

The lights menu (**U**) has the same patterns, traffic advisor, scene and takedown lights, stages, park mode and lens tint as [ELS: Light Bar AUS](/mods/els-light-bar/). The menu labels are in French when the game is.

The lights run in **Lite** mode: no shadows and a real LED beam shape, so they cost almost no FPS. Add [Lights: HQ Real Light](/mods/lights-hq/) for the full-quality look.

Thanks to the French Discord community for the feedback that shaped this version.

## Controls

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **K** | Siren on/off (the game's own key; ELS plays its own siren) |
| Hold **H** | Siren on: air horn. Siren off: a manual wail that winds up while held |
| **H H** (double-tap) | Next siren tone |
| **I** | Sirens menu |
| **U** | Lights menu |

In a menu, point with the mouse or scroll, then click or let go of the key to pick. Right-click goes back. The menus are in French when the game is.

:::note[What's in the zip]
A package: [ELS: Sirens](/mods/els/), the shared light bar (ELS: Light Bar Engine), [Beacons: Blue](/mods/brighter-beacons/) and its own France file (`pakchunk1014`) that turns the bar blue and adds the French sirens. It also needs [ROMF and Beacons: Light Engine](/mods/tonnie-core/). Everything is in the zip, and the [Mod Launcher](/mods/installing/) adds it for you.
:::

:::caution[Can't be used with]
[ELS: Light Bar AUS](/mods/els-light-bar/), [Beacons: Red](/mods/red-beacons/), [Beacons: Red & Blue](/mods/red-blue-beacons/) or the [QLD RFS: Fire Truck](/mods/qld-rfs-truck-reskin/). The Mod Launcher asks which one to keep.
:::

## Known limitations

- The lights and sirens are on your own screen only. In multiplayer, other players see and hear their own game's truck.
- The siren tones are synthesised, not recorded. A community vote on the game's Discord picks the default tone.
