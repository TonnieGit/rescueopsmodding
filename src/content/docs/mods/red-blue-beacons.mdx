Gives the main fire truck (the CCF) **red and blue** emergency lights, much brighter than the originals, and (new in v2) **real light** from every beacon. Left and right are as seen from the driver's seat:

- **Roof beacons:** red on the left, blue on the right, front and rear, shining through coloured domes.
- **Front grille lightheads:** blue on the left, red on the right.
- **Rear lightheads:** alternating red, blue, red, blue.
- **Real light:** every beacon lights up the ground, walls, trees and smoke around the truck, flashing in step with its own lens.
- Every lighthead flashes on **its own pattern**, with a soft glow that you see from the front.
- **Switched off:** the lenses are muted red or muted blue.

The lights key (**J**) works as normal. Install the [Free Camera](/mods/free-camera/) to film them like the clips on this page.

## What it changes

| | |
|---|---|
| Files | `pakchunk1007-Red_Blue_Beacons-Windows_P.*` (this colour) + [Beacon Base and Tonnie Core](/mods/tonnie-core/) |
| How | Beacon Base adds a real light to each of the truck's 10 beacons and recolours the lenses when the truck appears. This mod only holds the colours. |

## One colour at a time

**Brighter Beacons**, **Red Beacons** and **Red + Blue Beacons** colour the same lights, so install one. The Mod Manager and `Install.bat` offer to remove the other colours, and they also remove the old v1 beacon files (`pakchunk1004-Red_Beacons-Windows_P` / `pakchunk1004-Red_Blue_Beacons-Windows_P`). Installing by hand? Delete those first.

## Known limitations

- The four roof beacons **spin in step**, as in the original game.
- The lights are drawn by your own game, so in multiplayer each player sees them only if they have the mod installed.
- A game update can break the shared [Tonnie Core](/mods/tonnie-core/#after-a-game-update). If the game crashes while loading after an update, remove its three files until a new version is out.

[How to install](/mods/installing/).
