Gives the main fire truck (the CCF) **red and blue** emergency lights, much brighter than the originals. Left and right are as seen from the driver's seat:

- **Roof beacons:** red on the left, blue on the right, front and rear, shining through coloured domes.
- **Front grille lightheads:** blue on the left, red on the right.
- **Rear lightheads:** alternating red, blue, red, blue.
- Every lighthead flashes on **its own pattern**, with a soft glow that you see from the front.
- **Switched off:** the lenses are muted red or muted blue.

The lights key (**J**) works as normal.

## Red Beacons or Red + Blue: pick one

This mod replaces the same lights as [Red Beacons](/mods/red-beacons/), so install **one or the other**. Before installing, delete these from `Wildfire\Content\Paks` if you have them. Version 1.2 of the [QLD RFS Truck Reskin](/mods/qld-rfs-truck-reskin/) installed them too:

```text
pakchunk1004-Red_Beacons-Windows_P.pak
pakchunk1004-Red_Beacons-Windows_P.utoc
pakchunk1004-Red_Beacons-Windows_P.ucas
```

The [QLD RFS Truck Reskin](/mods/qld-rfs-truck-reskin/) v1.3 already includes this mod, so you don't need both downloads.

| Replaces | `S_CCF_FlashingLight_01`, `S_CCF_FlashingLightRotatingMesh_01`, `S_CCF_BeaconLightx2_01`, `S_CCF_BeaconLightx4_01` |
| Adds | Materials `M_TonnieBeacon`, `M_TonnieBeaconGlow`, `M_TonnieLens`, the function `MF_TonnieSocketColor` and six instances |

## Known limitations

- The beacons **glow but don't light up** the ground or walls around the truck. The game gives them no real light sources.
- The four roof beacons **spin in step**. The game spins them all at the same speed.
