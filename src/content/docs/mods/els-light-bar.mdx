A full-width **red and blue LED light bar** on the main fire truck (the CCF), in place of the two front roof beacons, with a **rear traffic advisor** stick. Every module flashes on its own and throws **real light** onto the road, walls and smoke. Everything is picked from the lights menu (**U**) while you drive.

- **Patterns:** Quad 2, Double, Quint, Road Runner and Slow Runner, or **Cycle** through them every 5 seconds.
- **Traffic advisor:** left, right or centre out, on the bar and the rear stick.
- **Scene lights:** white side lights left, right or both. **Takedown:** white front lights.
- **Stages** 1 to 3, **Park Mode** (a calmer pattern while you're stopped), **Lens Tint** (clear or red and blue lenses) and **Clear All**.

The game's own lights key still switches the bar on and off.

## Controls

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **K** | Siren on/off (the game's own key; ELS plays its own siren) |
| Hold **H** | Siren on: air horn. Siren off: a manual wail that winds up while held |
| **H H** (double-tap) | Next siren tone |
| **I** | Sirens menu |
| **U** | Lights menu (with the [ELS Light Bar](/mods/els-light-bar/)) |

In a menu, point with the mouse or scroll, then click or let go of the key to pick. Right-click goes back. The menus are in French when the game is.

:::note[Needs ELS]
The light bar runs through [ELS](/mods/els/) and [Tonnie Core](/mods/tonnie-core/) v2. Both are in the zip, and the [Mod Launcher](/mods/installing/) adds them for you.
:::

:::caution[Light bar or beacon colours]
The light bar replaces the two front roof beacons, so it doesn't mix with [Brighter](/mods/brighter-beacons/), [Red](/mods/red-beacons/) or [Red + Blue Beacons](/mods/red-blue-beacons/), or the [QLD RFS Truck Reskin](/mods/qld-rfs-truck-reskin/)'s beacons. The Mod Launcher and `Install.bat` ask before swapping.
:::

## Known limitations

- The lights are on your own screen only. In multiplayer, other players see their own game's beacons.
- Real lights cost some frame rate on older graphics cards. A lighter version is on the way.
