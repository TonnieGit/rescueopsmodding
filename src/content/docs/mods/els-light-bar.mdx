A full-width **red and blue LED light bar** (red on the left, blue on the right) on the main fire truck (the CCF), in place of the two front roof beacons, with a **rear traffic advisor** stick. Every module flashes on its own and throws **real light** onto the road, walls and smoke. Everything is picked from the lights menu (**U**) while you drive.

- **Patterns:** Quad 2, Double, Quint, Road Runner and Slow Runner, or **Cycle** through them every 5 seconds.
- **Traffic advisor:** left, right or centre out, on the bar and the rear stick.
- **Scene lights:** white side lights left, right or both. **Takedown:** white front lights.
- **Stages** 1 to 3, **Park Mode** (a calmer pattern while you're stopped), **Lens Tint** (tinted or clear lenses) and **Clear All**.

The game's own lights key still switches the bar on and off. It includes the [ELS: Sirens](/mods/els/).

**New in v2:**

- **Lite by default:** the real light no longer casts shadows and follows a real LED beam shape, so it costs almost no FPS, and the bar no longer floods the cab with light. Add [Lights: HQ Real Light](/mods/lights-hq/) for the full-quality look.
- A darker **tinted lens**, on by default (**Lens Tint** in the **U** menu switches to clear). The lens only glows in front of the lit modules.
- Renamed from ELS Light Bar. [ELS: Light Bar France](/mods/els-france/) is the all-blue version.

## Controls

| Key | Does |
|---|---|
| **K** | Siren on/off (the game's own key; ELS plays its own siren) |
| Hold **H** | Siren on: air horn. Siren off: a manual wail that winds up while held |
| **H H** (double-tap) | Next siren tone |
| **I** | Sirens menu |
| **U** | Lights menu |

In a menu, point with the mouse or scroll, then click or let go of the key to pick. Right-click goes back. The menus are in French when the game is.

:::note[What's in the zip]
The light bar runs through [ELS: Sirens](/mods/els/) and [ROMF](/mods/tonnie-core/). Both are in the zip, with the shared light bar files (ELS: Light Bar Engine) and a small AUS file (`pakchunk1015`) that does nothing in game: it lets the [Mod Launcher](/mods/installing/) tell the AUS and France bars apart. The launcher adds everything for you.
:::

:::tip[Light bar and beacon colours]
Add [Beacons: Red & Blue](/mods/red-blue-beacons/) to match the bar. It also works with [Beacons: Blue](/mods/brighter-beacons/) or [Beacons: Red](/mods/red-beacons/): the bar takes the place of the two front roof beacons, and the grille and rear beacons keep their own colour. The [QLD RFS: Fire Truck](/mods/qld-rfs-truck-reskin/) comes with the light bar, ELS: Sirens and Beacons: Red & Blue.
:::

:::caution[One light bar at a time]
Can't be used with [ELS: Light Bar France](/mods/els-france/). The Mod Launcher asks which one to keep.
:::

## Known limitations

- The lights are on your own screen only. In multiplayer, other players see their own game's beacons.
- In Lite mode the light casts no shadows. For shadows, light in smoke and fog and full bounce light, add [Lights: HQ Real Light](/mods/lights-hq/) (costs FPS).
