All of Tonnie's light mods ([Beacons: Blue](/mods/brighter-beacons/), [Red](/mods/red-beacons/) and [Red & Blue](/mods/red-blue-beacons/), [ELS: Light Bar AUS](/mods/els-light-bar/), [ELS: Light Bar France](/mods/els-france/) and the [QLD RFS: Fire Truck](/mods/qld-rfs-truck-reskin/)) now run in **Lite** mode by default. The real light no longer casts shadows and follows a real LED beam shape, so it costs almost no FPS. It also stops the light bar flooding the cab with light.

**Lights: HQ** turns them back up to full quality:

- **Shadows** on every light.
- **Light in smoke and fog.**
- **Full bounce light** off the ground and walls.

It looks best, but it costs FPS. On its own it does nothing: it changes the lights of the mods above.

:::note[Needs ROMF]
Lights: HQ starts through [ROMF](/mods/tonnie-core/), a small shared file. The [Mod Launcher](/mods/installing/) installs it for you, and the zip includes it too.
:::

:::tip[Lite or HQ?]
Start with Lite. If your frame rate has room to spare, add Lights: HQ. Untick it in the Mod Launcher to go back to Lite.
:::

## Known limitations

- The lights are on your own screen only. In multiplayer, other players see their own game's lights.
- HQ costs frame rate. On older graphics cards, stay on Lite.
