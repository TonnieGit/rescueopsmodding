Mods made by other players are on [Community mods](/mods/community/). Made one yourself? [Share it with Mod Dev](/mod-dev/).

The beacons, the Free Camera, First Person, ELS and Lights: HQ share two small parts, [ROMF and Beacons: Light Engine](/mods/tonnie-core/). The [Mod Launcher](/mods/installing/) installs them for you. Both ELS light bars take the place of the two front roof beacons: AUS works with any beacon colour, France comes with Beacons: Blue.

All the light mods run in **Lite** mode by default: no shadows and a real LED beam shape, so they cost almost no FPS. Add [Lights: HQ Real Light](/mods/lights-hq/) for the full-quality look.
