title: Upload rules
description: 'What can be uploaded through Mod Dev in the Rescue Ops Mod Launcher, and what happens when a mod breaks the rules.'
---

Every upload gets a quick safety check before it goes live. These are the things it checks. They're here so players can trust what they install, and so modders get credit for their own work.

## Your mod must

- **Be your own work**, or you have the author's permission to share everything in it. Credit anyone who helped in the description.
- **Not contain anything ripped from other games**: models, textures, sounds or music taken from another game, even reworked. Assets from the Rescue Ops game itself are fine, since mods change the game they belong to.
- **Be what it says**: the description says what it changes and how to use it.
- **Contain only Unreal mod files** (`.pak`, `.utoc`, `.ucas`) with their own names, so they never overwrite another mod's files or the game's own.
- **Do nothing harmful**: nothing that damages saves or PCs, collects data, or is designed to break other players' games.
- **Keep it clean**: no hateful, sexual or shocking content. Realistic emergency services content is fine.

## What happens otherwise

- An upload that breaks a rule doesn't go live. The reason is shown in your launcher under **MOD DEV > My mods**, so you can fix it and upload again.
- A live mod that's found to break a rule (for example after player reports) is taken down, and the launcher tells you why.
- Repeatedly uploading ripped or harmful content means your uploads stop being accepted.

## Reporting a mod

Players can click **Report** on any mod's details in the launcher. Reports go privately to the modding hub team, never to the mod's author. Use it for mods that don't work, contain someone else's work, or are harmful.

## Your files

When your mod goes live, its files and pictures are published on the hub's GitHub, where players' launchers download them. You can have a mod taken down at any time: message **Tonnie** on [Patreon](https://patreon.com/Tonnie001).
