The Rescue Ops Mod Launcher 2.0 is out. If you already have the launcher, it offers the update the next time you open it. New here? See [Installing mods](/mods/installing/).

## Mod Dev: share your own mods

Made a mod for Rescue Ops? Click **MOD DEV** in the launcher, pick an author name, and upload it with a picture and a description. We run a quick safety check on every upload, then it goes live, usually within 24 hours, in every player's launcher and on the new [Community mods](/mods/community/) page. Players install it in one click and rate it. There's no password: your account is a key on your PC, plus a recovery code to move it to another one. How it works: [Mod Dev: share your mods](/mod-dev/).

- **Updates**: send a new version from **My mods**. Everyone who has your mod gets it in their launcher.
- **Inbox**: players' bug reports, suggestions and thanks arrive in the launcher, and the dashboard tells you when your mod goes live.

## For everyone

- **Community mods** sit in the mod list next to everyone else's, with their author, ratings and downloads.
- **Feedback**: send a mod's developer a bug report, a suggestion or thanks from the mod's details.
- **Report**: tell the modding hub team about a mod that's broken, stolen or harmful.
- **Drag and drop** `.pak`, `.utoc`, `.ucas` or `.zip` files onto the launcher to add them to your library.
- **NEW** tags on mods that appeared since you last opened the launcher, and new sorting: **most downloaded**, **top rated** and **newest**.
- The dashboard only counts the mods you picked (shared files like Tonnie Core stay out of the way), and its two columns line up.
- After an update, the launcher shows what's new.

Your mods, settings and presets are kept when it updates. Uploads follow the [upload rules](/mod-dev/rules/); see [Privacy](/privacy/) for what Mod Dev stores.
