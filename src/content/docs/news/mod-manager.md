The [Rescue Ops Mod Manager](/mods/installing/) installs, updates and removes the hub mods for you:

- Finds your game through Steam.
- Tick the mods you want and click install.
- Shows when an update is available.
- Removes clashing mods (for example two beacon mods) before installing.

Every mod zip also has an `Install.bat` if you'd rather not use the app. See [Installing mods](/mods/installing/).
