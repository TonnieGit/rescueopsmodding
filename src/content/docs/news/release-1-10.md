## Lite by default

All the light mods (the beacons, the ELS light bar and the QLD RFS truck) now run in **Lite** mode. The real light no longer casts shadows and follows a real LED beam shape. That saves a lot of FPS, and the light bar no longer floods the cab with light.

Want the full look back? Add **[Lights: HQ Real Light](/mods/lights-hq/)**: shadows on every light, light in smoke and fog, and full bounce light. It costs FPS. On its own it does nothing.

## ELS: Light Bar France

**[ELS: Light Bar France](/mods/els-france/)** is out: an all-blue light bar, blue grille and rear beacons, and five French **deux-tons pompiers** sirens on the sirens menu (**I**). The menus are in French when the game is.

French players: please vote for the default siren tone on the Discord. Thanks to the French community for the feedback.

## Darker light bar lens

The light bar lens is now darker and **tinted by default**, and only glows in front of the lit modules. **Lens Tint** in the lights menu (**U**) switches to clear.
