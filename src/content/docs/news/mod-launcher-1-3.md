The Rescue Ops Mod Launcher 1.3 is out. If you already have the launcher, it offers the update the next time you open it. New here? See [Installing mods](/mods/installing/).

- **Presets.** Save the mods you have loaded as a preset and switch between sets in one click. **Base game** (no mods) is always there. The arrow next to **PLAY** starts the game with any preset.
- **Share codes.** Every preset has a code (`ROMOD-…`) you can paste on Discord. Anyone who imports it gets the same mods, and mods they don't have yet are downloaded first.
- **Star ratings.** Rate any mod you've downloaded, from 1 to 5 stars. The first time, the launcher asks for a name and email once. They stay private: only each mod's average and number of ratings are shown. See [Privacy](/privacy/).
- **Download counts** for every mod, in the launcher and on each mod's page here.
- **A new look.** Mods show as cards with big thumbnails; switch to the list view with **Grid | List**. Click a mod's picture to see its screenshots full size. The dashboard is simpler: your loaded mods as pictures, then the news.
- **Check and repair** (Options › Help) re-checks every mod file and downloads any that are damaged or missing.
- **Game update warnings.** After a game patch, loaded mods that haven't been checked on the new version are marked until they are.

Your mods, settings and loaded set are kept when it updates.
