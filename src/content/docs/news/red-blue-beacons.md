Two new mods for the CCF fire truck:

- [Red Beacons](/mods/red-beacons/): all-red, brighter emergency lights.
- [Red + Blue Beacons](/mods/red-blue-beacons/): red and blue, brighter, each lighthead flashing on its own pattern.

Want to make your own? See the [Emergency lights](/tutorials/emergency-lights/) and [Red and blue beacons](/tutorials/red-blue-beacons/) tutorials.
