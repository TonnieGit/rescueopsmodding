The **[QLD RFS Truck Reskin](/mods/qld-rfs-truck-reskin/)** is now version 1.5 and comes fitted out with ELS:

- The **[ELS Light Bar](/mods/els-light-bar/)**: a full-width red and blue LED light bar in place of the two front roof beacons, with a rear traffic advisor, flash patterns, scene and takedown lights (lights menu: **U**).
- **[ELS](/mods/els/)** sirens: recorded Hazard 1000 Wail, Yelp and Dual, plus Hi-Lo and Phaser, an air horn and a siren menu (**I**). These replace the old Hazard 1000 tones.
- The red and blue grille and rear beacons stay, still throwing real light.

The light bar now also works alongside Brighter, Red and Red + Blue Beacons on their own: it takes the front roof beacons, and the grille and rear beacons keep their colour.

Already have the truck? The [Mod Launcher](/mods/installing/) shows the update and removes the old Hazard 1000 files for you.
