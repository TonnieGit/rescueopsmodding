Two new mods for the main fire truck:

- **[ELS: Emergency Lighting and Sirens](/mods/els/)**: recorded Australian Hazard 1000 Wail, Yelp and Dual, plus Hi-Lo and Phaser, an air horn, a manual wail, Auto Cycle, a Rumbler and Low Volume, all from a siren menu (**I**). It leaves the lights alone, so it works with any beacon mod.
- **[ELS Light Bar](/mods/els-light-bar/)**: a full-width red and blue LED light bar with a rear traffic advisor. Five flash patterns plus Cycle, traffic advisor, scene and takedown lights, stages, park mode and lens tint, from the lights menu (**U**). Every module throws real light.

ELS replaces [Hazard 1000 Tones](/mods/hazard-1000-tones/), and needs the new [Tonnie Core](/mods/tonnie-core/) v2. The [Mod Launcher](/mods/installing/) sorts all of that out for you: tick the mod and click Play.
