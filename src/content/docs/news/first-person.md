**[Camera: First Person](/mods/first-person/)** is out. Press **B** in game to switch between the normal camera and first person.

![First person on foot: the hose stream hitting a line of fire](/media/first-person/nozzle.jpg)

- **On foot** the camera rides your firefighter's head, so it moves as you walk. Your own body is hidden, but its shadow stays.
- **In a fire truck** the camera sits in your seat and moves with the truck: driver, front passenger or the rear seats.
- **Hold Alt** to look around without turning your body, like in Arma or Squad. Double-tap Alt to lock free look on, tap it again to unlock.
- **Hold right mouse** to zoom.
- **Settings › General** in the pause menu gets two new rows: **First Person FOV** (70 to 120) and **First Person Head Bob**. Your settings are remembered.

It only changes your own screen: in multiplayer, other players see you as normal.

Get it in the [Mod Launcher](/mods/installing/), or download the zip from the [mod page](/mods/first-person/). It comes with **ROMF v3**, which every Blueprint mod zip now includes; the launcher updates it for you.
