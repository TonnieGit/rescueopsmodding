Enter the email address you gave in the Rescue Ops Mod Launcher. Your name, email, every rating you've given, and any feedback and reports you sent are deleted. The averages shown for each mod update to match.

<DeleteDetails />

The launcher on your PC still remembers your details. The next time you rate a mod, it asks for them again. To clear them from your PC as well, delete `%LOCALAPPDATA%\RescueOpsModLauncher\state.json`, which also resets the launcher's settings and presets.
