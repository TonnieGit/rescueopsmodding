This site and the Rescue Ops Mod Launcher are a free community project by Tonnie. They collect as little as possible.

## Ratings in the Mod Launcher

The first time you rate a mod, the launcher asks for a **name** and an **email address**. It sends them once to this site's database, together with:

- a **random install ID**: a number the launcher makes up the first time it starts, so your ratings can be told apart from everyone else's. It contains nothing about you or your PC.
- your **ratings** (1 to 5 stars per mod).
- a **one-way code made from your internet connection's address**. The address itself isn't kept. The code is only used to stop one connection making lots of accounts.

**What's public:** only each mod's average rating and how many ratings it has. Your name, email and individual ratings are never shown.

**What it's used for:** running ratings and, later, letting you upload your own mods from the launcher. Your email isn't used for newsletters or marketing, and it's never sold or given to anyone.

**Where it's kept:** in a database run by [Neon](https://neon.com/), connected to this site through [Vercel](https://vercel.com/), which hosts it. It's never put in the site's public code on GitHub.

The launcher also keeps your name, email and ratings on your own PC, in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\RescueOpsModLauncher\state.json`, so it doesn't ask again.

## Download counts

Download counts come from GitHub, which counts how many times each mod's zip has been downloaded. They're totals only. Nothing about who downloaded is involved.

## This site

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page visits. It doesn't use cookies or follow you across other sites.

## Having your details removed

**[Delete my details](/privacy/delete/)**: enter the email you gave and your name, email and ratings are deleted.

You can change your name or email at any time in the launcher: **Options › Launcher › Your details**. If anything goes wrong, message **Tonnie** on [Patreon](https://patreon.com/Tonnie001).
